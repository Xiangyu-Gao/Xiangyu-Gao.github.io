I am a Senior Software Engineer at Zoox in the Bay Area, CA. My current work focuses on vision-language models (VLMs) and vision-language-action models (VLAs) for physical AI. Previously, I was a Senior Research Engineer at PlusAI, where I was a core contributor to its VLA model, ranked #1 on the Waymo Open Dataset Vision-Based End-to-End Driving leaderboard (July 2026), and before that a machine learning engineer at Motional.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the University of Washington in 2023, advised by Prof. Sumit Roy at FUNLAB. My earlier research spans radar perception and multi-sensor fusion for autonomous driving, combining statistical signal processing with deep learning, as well as learning-based optimization for wireless and edge-computing networks.

Now every appointment I have is a Doctor's appointment.

Bio

Short bio (third person, for talks and introductions)

Xiangyu Gao is a Senior Software Engineer at Zoox in the Bay Area, CA. His current work focuses on vision-language models (VLMs) and vision-language-action models (VLAs) for physical AI. Previously, he was a Senior Research Engineer at PlusAI (2024–2026), where he was a core contributor to PlusAI's VLA model, ranked #1 on the Waymo Open Dataset Vision-Based End-to-End Driving leaderboard in July 2026, and a machine learning engineer at Motional (2023–2024). During his Ph.D., he interned at Volvo Cars (2021) and Intel (2022). He received his Ph.D. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the University of Washington in August 2023, advised by Prof. Sumit Roy at FUNLAB; his dissertation, Efficient and Enhanced Radar Perception for Autonomous Driving Systems, developed radar signal processing and deep learning methods for autonomous driving. His research has spanned radar perception, radar-camera fusion, and learning-based optimization for wireless and edge-computing networks, with 20+ publications and over 1,100 citations.

Xiangyu received a B.E. degree in telecommunication engineering from Xidian University, Xi’an, China, in 2018, and an M.S. degree in electrical and computer engineering from the University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA, in 2020. He also received an M.S. degree in Applied Mathematics from the University of Washington in 2023.
