I am a Senior Software Engineer at Zoox in the Bay Area, CA. My current work focuses on vision-language models (VLMs) and vision-language-action models (VLAs) for physical AI.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the University of Washington in 2023, advised by Prof. Sumit Roy at FUNLAB. Biographical Sketch

Xiangyu Gao is a Senior Software Engineer at Zoox in the Bay Area, CA. His current work focuses on vision-language models (VLMs) and vision-language-action models (VLAs) for physical AI. Previously, he was a machine learning engineer at Motional. He received his Ph.D. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the University of Washington in August 2023, advised by Prof. Sumit Roy at FUNLAB; his dissertation, Efficient and Enhanced Radar Perception for Autonomous Driving Systems, developed radar signal processing and deep learning methods for autonomous driving. His research has spanned radar perception, radar-camera fusion, and learning-based optimization for wireless and edge-computing networks, with 20+ publications and over 1,100 citations.

Xiangyu received a B.S. degree in communication engineering from Xidian University, Xi’an, China, in 2018, and an M.S. degree in electrical and computer engineering from the University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA, in 2020. He also received an M.S. degree in Applied Mathematics from the University of Washington in 2023.
